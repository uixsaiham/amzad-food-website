## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape editorial blog cover for Amzad Food's article 'A thoughtful pantry for everyday cooking'. Create a beautiful realistic Bangladeshi home kitchen pantry still life: clear glass jars of rice and red lentils, small ceramic bowls of turmeric, cumin and coriander, a small bottle of golden cooking oil, wooden spoon and folded natural linen on a warm wooden counter. Soft daylight, muted sage wall, warm earthy colors, tactile authentic materials. Landscape 3:2 composition, all principal ingredients within the central area so it also crops well to 16:9. A restrained food magazine photograph, no people, no typography, no logos, no packaging, no promotional stickers, no supplements.
